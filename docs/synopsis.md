# Synopsis

Built from the script as batches land (B = main chapters B00-B39, A = detour scenes A<chapter>.<n>). Extend it each
session; translators read it before their batch.

## Cast
- The player ({name2} {name1}, default Tatsuma Hiyuu): a high-school boy whose soul drifts in a vortex of darkness
  after (apparently) dying in hospital. Enra pulls him out and makes him the new Ujigami (clan guardian god) of the
  Yamura family. He fights by drawing Thoughts from memories (the cards).
- Shun Yamura (矢村俊): a Summoner (勧請師), stiff and formal, insists on respect for the Ujigami.
- Isuzu Mizukane (水兼伊涼): shrine maiden with the Divine Bell (神鈴), casual, cheeky ({name1}-kun).
- Enra (焔羅): the old Ujigami ("Great Deity Enra"), gruff old-man speech; leaves to fulfil his own role.
- Woman's Voice (B00-B01): a woman's soul in the darkness who recites Hagiwara's poem; promises to meet again.

## Chapters
- B00: hospital (CPR on the player) intercut with the darkness; the Woman's Voice; evil spirits attack.
- B01: spirits scatter; a bell rings; the Woman's Voice leaves toward the light.
- B02: Enra wakes the player in Tokyo; crows maddened by Miasma attack Shun and Isuzu on a slope; Shun summons the
  Ujigami and gets the player; first battle.
- B03: Enra leaves the player in charge; introductions; the slope's Miasma is behind serial street killings. A
  woman on the slope (dead; ate her stillborn baby's remains, wants it "back in her belly") is the killer; battle.
- B04: Isuzu seals her Miasma (邪氣封印 "Miasma, be sealed!"); she confesses her Sin and passes on. Yamura: no one is
  without Sin, so anyone can be possessed. They take the player to the Yamura family's old shrine (祠), which heals
  an Ujigami's Ki and hides something useful. Isuzu goes casual ({name1}-kun / {name2}-chan); Yamura switches from
  -sama to {name1}-dono. Coda: Enra confronts a Shadow / Woman's Voice ("for the souls of THEM", 《彼ら》).
  Girl-player branch: rows with {name2}-chan and B04.0174 (老爺と少女 "an old man and a girl") imply the player can be
  a girl; keep player lines gender-neutral where the JP is.
- A0.x-A3.x: the same detour format, mostly villains and cast of the Majin series musing on a theme (judging the
  world, oni and Desire, jealousy, love, money, friendship), each answer branch ending differently. The five
  Retainers (雷/風/水/土/炎の従者) are oni of the Kidoshu (鬼道衆). Maiden of the Peach Garden is a sentai Pink who
  calls the player "Green".
- A4.x / A5.x: detour chats with one recurring card character each (speaker tag = their title), with player
  choices; each ends differently per answer. Firerunner = Marie (マリィ, katakana child speech, calls the player
  Onii-chan); Spirit Gunner = broken-English foreigner (render as "-desu", "ne", caps).
- B05: an elderly couple (Elder, Old Woman), possessed by Miasma, speak things into Fact: they trap a land-grabber
  in an invisible prison (the "spirited away" 神隠し cases, seven in a month). Morning at the shrine; asking around
  by the river (choice: Night Worker / Old Folks / Office Worker); the couple summon a pack of mad dogs.
- B06: the dogs vanish; the couple boast of their Power ("speaking things into Fact"); battle.
- B07: Isuzu seals their Miasma; the couple confess their loneliness (family gone, ignored by society); Yamura and
  Isuzu offer to help them change things. Isuzu asks the player's old school: Magami Academy (真神学園) in Shinjuku,
  which neither has heard of. Yamura's Summoning only lasts so long each day.
- B08: a comatose boy vanishes from a hospital's seventh floor; a giant bird causes a pile-up; people collapse in
  Kabukicho. The Boy (speeding biker, eagle fixation, wants a "nuclear warhead" of Power to wreck the rotten world)
  smells the player's Power, drags the player out of the Yamura shrine to Kabukicho and demands it; battle. Girl
  branch: B08.0199 (キミは…女ではない) calls the player a girl.
- B09: the beaten Boy refuses to give up and turns into a Great Eagle ("the top of the food chain"); battle.
- B10: the Boy's Power turns on him; the player subdues it. Isuzu finds the player collapsed outside the shrine
  and seals the Miasma in a talisman (girl branch: "You're a girl, and you can fight alone"). In hospital, the
  comatose boy is back in bed, calmer. Coda: the Woman's Voice overpowers Enra, who tried to destroy her before she
  gains a Vessel (器); she vows to walk the earth again and makes Enra "help".
- A11.x detours: the Songstress (TV idol, "nobody knows the real me"), the Maiden of the Fragrant Breeze (wants to
  rush through cleaning a room; quarrels with the player's attitude).
- B11: Azusa, a girl who fed a stray dog, finds it beaten to death by two men; her father is a thief, her mother
  scorns her; she kills them both and then herself. Yamura (on an errand for his father: one rare piece of stationery
  in Shinjuku) and Isuzu see Azusa's corpse after a "random stabbing" by a middle-aged couple: no blood, Miasma.
  Azusa's spirit calls them by Ki up 340 stone steps to a ruined shrine guarded by Akagane, a huntsman with dogs who
  protects her only inside the shrine's Power Field. Her parents' vengeful ghosts (Eerie Man/Woman, "parent-killer")
  attack; Akagane, they say, also died an unjust death. Yamura's talisman stops them; battle.
- A12.x detours: Courier (Koshu Kaido runner), Aesthete (poet: "be this world's last poet"), Baptized Maiden (doubts
  her prayers), Little Fist (anger), Cat Girl (dreamed of someone like the player), Cursed Blade Wielder (killing and
  a person's "place"), Kunoichi (stalker "confession", then says it was a lie), One-Eyed Tengu (only happiness),
  Foxfire Courtesan (love turning to jealousy, "the heart turns oni"), Automaton Maker (the pain and joy of making).
- B12: the parents' ghosts rot away. Akagane explains Azusa is long dead; the Great Sin revived her and her parents
  as a pair, so sealing it means sealing her too. Choice: hand Akagane the talisman or not; he takes it (his dog
  snatches it), absorbs the parents' half of the Great Sin to draw out Azusa's half, and turns into a beast (he was
  never human). Azusa offers her Thought to the Ujigami; Isuzu and Yamura tell her she's too weak; battle.
- B13: Akagane reveals he was the stray dog Azusa wept over; her kindness kept his soul from hatred, and this is his
  repayment. He is sealed with the Great Sin, promising to find his way back to her. Yamura vows to wipe every
  Miasma out of Tokyo.
- B14: Isuzu dreams of a battlefield of skulls: a Young Warrior, Toshimune (looks like Shun), stands over the
  defeated Takamaru, whose Power an Ujigami (Enra) sealed; Takamaru swears to return and take the heads of
  Toshimune's children and grandchildren; a woman whispers "Toshimune". Takamaru appears to Isuzu: she is the "Oni
  Princess of Suzuka", of his kind; he summons a shikigami (a Woman's Voice promised a new body for Toshimune's
  head). The Woman's Voice, a Shadow assassin, wakes Yamura ("Toshimune"); he summons the player; battle.
- B15: the beaten assassin says Isuzu went to "the land where grudges sleep, below the sixty-tiered tower" to
  become her true self. Yamura reads it as Sunshine 60 over Ikebukuro (old Sugamo Prison), blames himself, and asks
  the player to come. In Ikebukuro, vengeful voices of "the clan of Mt. Oe" attack him as Toshimune; battle.
- B16: the Mt. Oe voices were Shuten and Tamamo, souls revived from Uji's treasure house. Takamaru shows Yamura the
  war of 1200 years ago: outcasts ("the Unyielding") branded the Shade by villagers who called themselves the Light;
  their hatred became curses and Power; Shade warred on Light (Omi, Mt. Suzuka, Ezo). Underground, with Isuzu asleep,
  Takamaru names Yamura the reincarnation of Fujiwara no Toshimune, the Oni-Hunter General who took his head three
  times and killed his daughter, and Isuzu the reincarnation of Suzuka Gozen, the Oni Princess who betrayed the
  Shade for Toshimune. He argues the Great Sin is simply the Power of today's oppressed Shade folk, tells the player
  "you're no soul of this world" and an outsider; battle.
- B17: beaten, Takamaru warns history will repeat ("watch every step or you'll lose something big again") and
  escapes. Isuzu wakes unhurt but blames herself for failing to seal his Miasma; Yamura comforts her (branches by
  the player's reply: harsh words lead Yamura to ask her for the will never to give up).
- B18: a library in search of Takamaru's past; the player picks Yamura's or Isuzu's half (choice B18.0113).
  Yamura route: a picture book (Tamuramaro and Princess Suzuka beat the oni) and a study of Mt. Suzuka: Otakemaru,
  a Heian rebel with demon power who planned a "demon nation", was killed by the young general Fujiwara no Toshimune
  with the celestial maiden Suzuka Gozen, whom he married; Otakemaru revived and was killed again. Yamura admits he
  half agrees with Takamaru and wishes Isuzu didn't have to fight. Isuzu route: she recalls how it began two months
  ago (a cursed doll in a shrine; Enra saved them and woke their Power) and finds "The Legend of Akuro-o": the
  Court branded frontier peoples like the Emishi savage rebels, just like Takamaru's Shade folk. Both routes: the
  Kokon Chomonju says Toshimune later killed Suzuka Gozen for her oni nature; two men (Man on the Left/Right) speak
  through the Word Spirits, calling it a Sin to deny words as lies, and raise an uprising of the Word Spirits; battle.
- B19: Isuzu arrives in time; the Word Spirits, a Great Sin, cry "acknowledge us"; battle.
- B20: the Word Spirits fade and the book goes blank. Yamura sums up: Otakemaru, a 1200-year-old oni of Mt. Suzuka
  killed by Toshimune with Suzuka Gozen's help, matches Takamaru. He keeps secret that Toshimune later killed Suzuka
  ("As if I could tell her"). Elsewhere, Shadows on the Left and Right (whose master's Ki has vanished) see the
  Great Sin gathering to one point and send Uhatsura, ready to wet their hands with human blood.
- B21: Yamura and Isuzu share a dream: a dying man with an arrow through his heart blames Isuzu ("you saw through
  the truth"); his death freezes everything. Uhatsura says the scene comes from Isuzu's own depths and offers a
  deal: all the sealed Great Sin plus Isuzu's life (a "human pillar") for Yamura's and the player's release. Isuzu
  agrees; the player may hand over the talismans. Yamura (or the player) breaks the ice wall; battle.
