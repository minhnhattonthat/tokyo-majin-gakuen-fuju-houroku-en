## Cast
- The player ({name2} {name1}, default Tatsuma Hiyuu): a high-school boy whose soul drifts in a vortex of darkness
  after (apparently) dying in hospital. Enra pulls him out and makes him the new Ujigami (clan guardian god) of the
  Yamura family. He fights by drawing Thoughts from memories (the cards).
- Shun Yamura (矢村俊): a Summoner (勧請師), stiff and formal, insists on respect for the Ujigami.
- Isuzu Mizukane (水兼伊涼): shrine maiden with the Divine Bell (神鈴), casual, cheeky ({name1}-kun).
- Enra (焔羅): the old Ujigami ("Great Deity Enra"), gruff old-man speech; leaves to fulfil his own role.
- Woman's Voice (B00-B01): a woman's soul in the darkness who recites Hagiwara's poem; promises to meet again.

## Chapters

- B01: spirits scatter; a bell rings; the Woman's Voice leaves toward the light.
- B02: Enra wakes the player in Tokyo; crows maddened by Miasma attack Shun and Isuzu on a slope; Shun summons the
  Ujigami and gets the player; first battle.
- B03: Enra leaves the player in charge; introductions; the slope's Miasma is behind serial street killings.
